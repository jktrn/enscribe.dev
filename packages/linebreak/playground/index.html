Metrics

Lines, spaces and badness are measured from rendered glyph rectangles in this browser. Hyphenated-break counts for the two typeset columns are read from engine markup, and the browser's from words painted in two rectangles. Badness is TeX's 100·|r|³, capped at 10,000, over body lines only, from each line's own measured slack. A space beside an inline code chip carries that chip's padding, in every column alike.
