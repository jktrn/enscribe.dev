As a kid raised by internet forums, I was obsessed with customizing my signatures and &ldquo;about me&rdquo; sections on personal profiles using [BBCode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBCode) (you can add `<dim>[</dim><white>img</white><dim>]</dim>cool-images.png<dim>[/</dim><white>img</white><dim>]</dim>` and `<dim>[</dim><white>b</white><dim>]</dim>bold text<dim>[/</dim><white>b</white><dim>]</dim>`!). I loved how constrained the environment was and attempted to squeeze as much expression and personality into it as possible. I used these [gradient generator tools](https://www.stuffbydavid.com/textcolorizer) to add <span class="awesome-colors">awesome colors</span>, threw in these weird cursive <abbr title="Unicode characters">𝒰𝓃𝒾𝒸𝑜𝒹𝑒 𝒸𝒽𝒶𝓇𝒶𝒸𝓉𝑒𝓇𝓈</abbr>, and made perfectly dimensioned GIF art with [Ezgif](https://ezgif.com/). To this day, the web is still my favorite design medium.

Although I ended up studying data science for my undergraduate, as a childhood dream of mine I wanted to design on the internet. To that end, I'm now a [design engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_engineer#In_software_engineering)! I'm currently at [OtterSec](https://osec.io), a security firm, where I've worked with teams like the [Solana Fdn.](https://solana.org/) and [OpenAI/Paradigm](https://openai.com/index/introducing-evmbench/). Outside of work, some of my collaborations include [V12](https://v12.sh), [Zellic](https://zellic.io), [Hacktron](https://hacktron.ai), [Veria Labs](https://verialabs.com/) (YC F25), and the [Ethereum Fdn.](https://ethereum.foundation/)

You might notice that the majority of my work pertains to cybersecurity in some aspect. I am heavily involved in the [capture-the-flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capture_the_flag_(cybersecurity)) community, where I organize events and occasionally author challenges for [Project Sekai CTF](https://sekai.team) and the [Friendly Maltese Citizens](https://ctf.mt). If you want to see my work, check out my [/gallery](/gallery)!

Outside of my profession, music is everything to me. I love discovering [new albums](/music), [attending concerts](/music#concerts), writing arrangements, and practicing on my beloved electric and classical guitars---I own a [Schecter Omen-6 Extreme FR LH](https://www.guitarcenter.com/Schecter-Guitar-Research/Omen-Extreme-6-FR-Left-Handed-Electric-Guitar-Black-Cherry-1337871392897.gc) and a [Cordoba C5-CE Lefty](https://cordobaguitars.com/iberia/c5-ce-lefty/) (yes, I'm a lefty ꃋᴖꃋ). You'll also often see me frequent the video arcade after work to play rhythm games (I mainly play [maimai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maimai_(video_game_series)) and [In the Groove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_the_Groove_(video_game)), similar to <abbr title="Dance Dance Revolution">DDR</abbr>).

If we happen to be in the same place, I'm always down to grab coffee or say hi. Although I was born and raised in Los Angeles (and you're most likely to find me there), I'm occasionally around San Diego, San Francisco, and New York City. I also try to attend [SECCON](https://www.seccon.jp/) in Tokyo and (begrudgingly) [DEF CON](https://defcon.org/) in Las Vegas every year. Feel free to message me!
