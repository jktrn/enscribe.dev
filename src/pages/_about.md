As a kid raised by internet forums, I was obsessed with customizing my signatures and &ldquo;about me&rdquo; sections on personal profiles using [BBCode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBCode) (you can add `<dim>[</dim><white>img</white><dim>]</dim>cool-images.png<dim>[/</dim><white>img</white><dim>]</dim>` and `<dim>[</dim><white>b</white><dim>]</dim>bold text<dim>[/</dim><white>b</white><dim>]</dim>`!). I loved how constrained the environment was and attempted to squeeze as much expression and personality into it as possible. I used these [gradient generator tools](https://www.stuffbydavid.com/textcolorizer) to add <span style='color:#FFC9C9;'>a</span><span style='color:#FFCBC4;'>w</span><span style='color:#FFCEBF;'>e</span><span style='color:#FFD1BA;'>s</span><span style='color:#FFD4B5;'>o</span><span style='color:#FFD6B0;'>m</span><span style='color:#FFD9AB;'>e</span> <span style='color:#FFDFA2;'>c</span><span style='color:#FFE29D;'>o</span><span style='color:#FFE498;'>l</span><span style='color:#FFE793;'>o</span><span style='color:#FFEA8E;'>r</span><span style='color:#FFED89;'>s</span>, threw in these weird cursive <abbr title="Unicode characters">𝒰𝓃𝒾𝒸𝑜𝒹𝑒 𝒸𝒽𝒶𝓇𝒶𝒸𝓉𝑒𝓇𝓈</abbr>, and made perfectly dimensioned GIF art with [Ezgif](https://ezgif.com/). As such, the web is still my favorite design medium today.

Although I ended up studying data science for my undergraduate, as a childhood dream of mine I wanted to design on the internet. To that end, I work as a [design engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_engineer#In_software_engineering)! I'm currently a full-timer at [OtterSec](https://osec.io), a security auditing firm in the Web3 space. I feel incredibly privileged and lucky to be able to express myself creatively for a living.

You might notice that the majority of my work pertains to cybersecurity in some aspect. I am heavily involved in the [capture-the-flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capture_the_flag_(cybersecurity)) community, where I organize events and occasionally author challenges for [Project Sekai CTF](https://sekai.team) and the [Friendly Maltese Citizens](https://ctf.mt). Other than that, I've [dabbled in AI academia](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15216) with some friends at Stanford University. If you want to see what I've done, check out my [/work](/work) page.

Outside of my profession, music is a huge part of my life. I love discovering [new albums](/music), [attending concerts](/music#concerts), writing arrangements, and practicing on my beloved electric and classical guitars---I own a [Schecter Omen-6 Extreme FR LH](https://www.guitarcenter.com/Schecter-Guitar-Research/Omen-Extreme-6-FR-Left-Handed-Electric-Guitar-Black-Cherry-1337871392897.gc) and a [Cordoba C5 Lefty](https://cordobaguitars.com/iberia/c5-lefty/) (yes, I'm a lefty ꃋᴖꃋ). You'll also often see me frequent the video arcade after work to play rhythm games (I mainly play [maimai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maimai_(video_game_series)) and have been recently learning [In the Groove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_the_Groove_(video_game)), similar to <abbr title="Dance Dance Revolution">DDR</abbr>).
